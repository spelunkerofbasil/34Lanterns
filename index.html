    About this piece

    A quiet place to set something down. Lanterns drift on still water and rise into the night sky, each carrying something a person chose to let go of: a doubt, a wish, a promise, a fear. Read the ones already adrift, or write your own and release it.

    How it works

    • Tap or click a lantern to read its note. Keyboard users can press Tab to reach the lantern list.
    • Type a note and press Release (or Ctrl/⌘+Enter).
    • Inspire offers a prompt if you are not sure what to write.
    • Your lanterns are saved only in this browser on this device. No one else can see them.
    • Notes are anonymous. No name, email or location is collected.
