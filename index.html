About this piece

Lanterns drift on still waters and rise across the starspangled sky. Click a lantern to read the note attached to it. Create a new lantern by typing, then press Release.

How it works

  • Lanterns drift left→right or rise upward with subtle bobbing.
  • Click a lantern to open its note near where it floats.
  • Press Inspire for a prompt, then Release to add your lantern.
  • Notes you add are saved locally in your browser (no server).
  • Use Private mode to avoid saving new notes.
